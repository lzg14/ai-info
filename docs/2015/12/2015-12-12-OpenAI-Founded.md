# OpenAI Founded: A New Era of Safe AI Development

📅 2015-12-12 | 📎 OpenAI Official | ⭐ 精选

<!-- 正文开始 -->
On December 12, 2015, OpenAI was officially founded with a mission to ensure that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits humanity. The organization was announced with over $1 billion in backing from prominent technology leaders and entrepreneurs, including Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Peter Thiel, and Amazon Web Services. This represented one of the largest initial funding commitments in the history of artificial intelligence research.

The founding principles of OpenAI emphasized safety, transparency, and broad distribution of AI benefits. Unlike traditional research labs that operate behind closed doors, OpenAI committed to publishing its research openly and making its code available to the public. This approach was designed to prevent the concentration of AI power in the hands of a few corporations and to establish safety standards for the entire industry.

OpenAI's research agenda focused on advancing AI capabilities while simultaneously developing robust safety mechanisms. The organization recognized that as AI systems became more powerful, ensuring their safe development and deployment would become increasingly critical. This dual focus on capability and safety became a defining characteristic of the lab's approach.

The founding team brought together researchers from some of the world's leading institutions, including Google Brain, DeepMind, Stanford University, and Carnegie Mellon University. This concentration of talent created immediate credibility and positioned OpenAI as a serious competitor to established AI research labs at major technology companies.

In its inaugural year, OpenAI quickly established itself as a significant player in the AI research community. The organization began recruiting top talent, building research infrastructure, and initiating projects that would eventually produce groundbreaking results in reinforcement learning, natural language processing, and robotics. The launch of OpenAI represented a bold experiment in collaborative, safety-focused AI development that would reshape the landscape of artificial intelligence research in the years to come.
