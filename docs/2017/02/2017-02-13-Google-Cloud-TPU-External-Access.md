---
title: Google Cloud TPU Services Open to External Developers
date: 2017-02-13
source: Google Cloud Blog
url: https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/gcp/google-cloud-offerings-for-machine-learning
---

Google announced in February 2017 that its Tensor Processing Units (TPUs), the custom ASIC chips designed specifically for machine learning workloads, would become available through Google Cloud Platform to external developers and enterprises. This announcement marked a significant shift in Google's strategy, as the powerful AI acceleration hardware that had been developed internally for use in products like Search and Translate was now accessible to a broader range of users.

The Cloud TPU service allowed developers to leverage the same hardware that powered Google's own deep learning applications without having to invest in and maintain their own specialized infrastructure. This was particularly valuable for organizations that wanted to run large-scale machine learning training or inference workloads but lacked the resources or expertise to build and operate their own GPU clusters.

Google's decision to open up TPU access reflected the growing importance of specialized AI hardware in the technology industry. The competition among cloud providers to offer the most powerful and cost-effective machine learning infrastructure was intensifying, with Amazon and Microsoft also investing heavily in GPU-based services and custom AI chips.

The Cloud TPU offering came with integration into popular machine learning frameworks including TensorFlow, Google's open-source deep learning framework. This tight integration made it relatively straightforward for developers already using TensorFlow to migrate their workloads to the specialized hardware and benefit from significant acceleration in training times for large neural networks.

Google also introduced tiered pricing options for Cloud TPU access, including a beta version that allowed researchers and developers to experiment with the technology at reduced cost. This approach reflected Google's strategy to build a broader ecosystem around its AI infrastructure offerings while continuing to advance the state of the art in AI hardware.

The availability of Cloud TPUs represented an important moment in democratizing access to cutting-edge AI technology. Smaller organizations and individual researchers could now access the same computational power that had previously been available only to large technology companies with significant hardware engineering capabilities.
