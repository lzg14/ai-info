Microsoft Advances AI Research with New Deep Learning Tools and Azure Services

Microsoft Research Blog2017-06-15入库 2017-06-15

Microsoft continued its commitment to advancing artificial intelligence research and democratizing AI technologies throughout 2017, announcing significant improvements to its Azure Machine Learning platform and releasing new tools for deep learning researchers and developers. These efforts reflected the company's strategic focus on making AI more accessible and powerful for users across different skill levels.

The Azure Machine Learning updates included the introduction of automated machine learning capabilities that could help developers and data scientists more quickly build and deploy predictive models. By automating aspects of model selection and hyperparameter tuning, Microsoft aimed to reduce the time and expertise required to create effective machine learning solutions.

Microsoft Research also released updates to its Cognitive Toolkit (CNTK), an open-source deep learning framework that competed with TensorFlow, PyTorch, and other popular tools. The new releases improved performance, made the framework easier to use, and added support for new model architectures and training techniques that had emerged from cutting-edge research.

The company's investments in AI hardware partnerships and optimized runtimes for AI workloads on Azure demonstrated its understanding that practical AI deployment required careful attention to the entire stack, from algorithms to underlying infrastructure. These optimizations could significantly reduce costs and improve responsiveness for production AI applications.

Beyond technical advances, Microsoft emphasized its principles for responsible AI development, including fairness, reliability, privacy, and transparency. The company actively worked to address potential concerns about AI's impact on jobs and society, engaging with policymakers and stakeholders to ensure that AI technologies were developed and deployed responsibly.
