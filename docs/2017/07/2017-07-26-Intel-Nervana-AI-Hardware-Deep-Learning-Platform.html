Intel Nervana AI Hardware Platform Targets Deep Learning Performance Leadership

Intel AI Blog2017-07-26入库 2017-07-26

Intel announced its Nervana AI hardware platform in 2017, marking the company's strategic entry into the competitive market for AI-specific computing hardware. The platform was designed from the ground up to address the unique computational requirements of deep learning workloads, offering promising advances in both training and inference performance.

The centerpiece of Intel's AI hardware strategy was the Nervana Neural Network Processor (NNP), which featured a novel architecture optimized for the matrix multiplications and other operations that form the backbone of neural network training and inference. The processor was designed to scale across multiple chips and systems, addressing the growing computational demands of training increasingly large and complex models.

Intel's approach emphasized flexibility and programmability, allowing the hardware to support a wide range of neural network architectures and emerging AI models. This was particularly important as the AI field continued to evolve rapidly, with new architectures and training techniques constantly being developed that required adaptable hardware solutions.

The integration of Intel's hardware with its software ecosystem, including optimizations for popular deep learning frameworks like TensorFlow and PyTorch, aimed to make the platform accessible to developers without requiring significant code changes. The company also invested in tools for model conversion and deployment that could help users transition from training to production inference.

Intel's AI hardware initiatives reflected the broader industry trend toward specialization in AI computing, as general-purpose CPUs could no longer efficiently meet the demands of modern deep learning workloads. The competition between Intel, NVIDIA, and other chipmakers in this space promised continued innovation that would benefit AI applications across industries.
