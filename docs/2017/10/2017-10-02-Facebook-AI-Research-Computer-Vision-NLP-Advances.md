Facebook AI Research (FAIR) continued to make significant contributions to the AI community throughout 2017, releasing open-source tools and publishing research that advanced the state of the art in computer vision, natural language processing, and other areas of artificial intelligence. The lab's commitment to open research and collaboration with the broader academic community remained defining characteristics of its approach.

In computer vision, FAIR presented research on image segmentation, object detection, and video analysis that built on the success of deep learning approaches. The development of new architectures and training techniques allowed for more accurate and efficient visual recognition systems that could operate at scale on Facebook's massive platform.

Natural language processing research at FAIR focused on understanding and generating text, with work on machine translation, text classification, and conversational AI. The lab released improvements to fairseq, their open-source sequence-to-sequence modeling toolkit, which enabled researchers worldwide to build on their work and apply these techniques to their own applications.

FAIR also explored the intersection of multiple AI capabilities, including work on multimodal learning that combined visual and textual information. Research on image captioning, visual question answering, and other tasks that required integrating different types of AI demonstrated the importance of building systems that could understand and reason about multiple modalities.

The lab's approach to AI safety and responsible AI development was reflected in research on model interpretability, fairness in machine learning, and understanding potential failure modes of AI systems. As these technologies reached billions of users, FAIR recognized the importance of addressing potential risks and ensuring that AI was developed responsibly.
