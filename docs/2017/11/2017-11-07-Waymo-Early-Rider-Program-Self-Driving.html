Waymo Launches Early Rider Program for Self-Driving Car Service

Waymo2017-11-07入库 2017-11-07

Waymo officially launched its Early Rider Program in November 2017, marking a significant step toward commercializing self-driving vehicle technology. The program invited members of the public in the Phoenix, Arizona area to use Waymo's self-driving vehicles for their daily transportation needs, providing real-world feedback that would help refine the technology before a broader commercial launch.

Participants in the Early Rider Program could use a smartphone app to summon a Waymo self-driving vehicle to take them wherever they needed to go within a designated service area. The vehicles were equipped with Level 4 autonomous driving capabilities, meaning they could handle most driving situations without human intervention, though a Waymo employee would be present in the vehicle to monitor performance and take over if necessary.

The program built on Waymo's extensive testing experience, which included millions of miles driven on public roads and billions of miles simulated. Waymo had developed particularly sophisticated lidar, camera, and radar perception systems, along with prediction and planning software that could handle complex traffic situations including unprotected left turns, merging onto highways, and navigating construction zones.

Waymo's approach emphasized safety as the foundation of its technology development. The company had developed extensive testing protocols, redundancy systems, and operational design domains that clearly defined where and when their vehicles could operate safely. The Early Rider Program allowed Waymo to validate these safety systems with real passengers in real-world conditions.

The launch of the Early Rider Program represented a milestone in the autonomous vehicle industry, demonstrating that self-driving technology had progressed to the point where it could be tested with members of the public in actual use cases. The data and feedback gathered from the program would be invaluable for refining the technology and preparing for broader commercial deployment.
