# Stanford AI Index 2017 Shows Accelerating AI Progress and Investment

📅 2017-12-01 | 📎 Stanford HAI

<!-- 正文开始 -->
The inaugural AI Index report published in December 2017 provided a comprehensive assessment of the state of artificial intelligence, documenting rapid progress across multiple dimensions including research activity, industry investment, and societal adoption. The report, produced by the AI Index project at Stanford University in collaboration with partners from academia and industry, aimed to provide objective measurement of AI progress to inform policy decisions and public discourse.

The report documented the dramatic growth in AI research publications, with the number of AI-related papers increasing significantly year over year. However, it also noted that the definition of what constituted AI research was itself evolving, making simple publication counts imperfect measures of progress. The report tracked multiple metrics including conference attendance, patent filings, and venture capital investment to build a comprehensive picture of AI activity.

One notable finding was the increasing concentration of AI activity in industry versus academia. Large technology companies were investing billions of dollars in AI research and development, attracting talent away from academic institutions and conducting research at scales that were difficult to match in university settings. This shift raised questions about the future of fundamental AI research and the training of the next generation of AI researchers.

The report also addressed the economic impact of AI, documenting adoption across industries and estimating the potential for job displacement and creation. While the full economic implications remained uncertain, the report noted that AI was already transforming workflows and business models in sectors ranging from healthcare to financial services.

The AI Index project was designed to be an ongoing effort, with annual reports tracking progress over time. The 2017 report established baselines and methodologies that would allow for meaningful comparisons in future years, contributing to more informed discussions about AI and its implications for society.

### Stanford AI Index 2017（评分: 9.0/10）
