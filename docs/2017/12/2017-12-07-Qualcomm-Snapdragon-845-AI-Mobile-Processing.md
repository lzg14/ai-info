The Qualcomm Snapdragon 845, announced in December 2017, represented a significant leap forward in mobile AI processing capabilities. This new mobile processor introduced a dedicated AI engine that transformed how smartphones could handle artificial intelligence tasks, marking a new chapter in the competition among mobile chip makers to bring advanced AI capabilities to consumer devices.

The Snapdragon 845 featured the Hexagon 685 DSP, which served as the primary AI processing unit for on-device machine learning workloads. This dedicated AI hardware enabled smartphones to run neural network inference directly on the device, reducing latency and improving privacy by keeping data local rather than sending it to cloud servers for processing. The architecture was designed to handle a wide range of AI tasks including image recognition, natural language processing, and augmented reality applications.

Compared to its predecessor, the Snapdragon 835, the 845 delivered approximately three times better AI performance through the combination of improved CPU cores, an enhanced GPU, and the dedicated neural processing unit. This improvement was particularly important for applications like camera features, where AI could be used for scene detection, portrait mode effects, and real-time image enhancement without draining battery life or requiring network connectivity.

The competitive landscape for AI chips in smartphones was intensifying during this period, with Apple introducing its Neural Engine in the A11 Bionic chip and Huawei launching the Kirin 970 with a dedicated NPU. Qualcomm's approach with the Snapdragon 845 emphasized flexibility, supporting multiple AI frameworks including TensorFlow, Caffe, and ONNX, allowing developers to deploy their machine learning models across different platforms without significant modification.

The Snapdragon 845 also featured advances in wireless connectivity with the X20 modem supporting Gigabit LTE, improved security with a dedicated Secure Processing Unit, and enhanced camera capabilities with the Spectra 280 ISP capable of supporting video recording at ultra-high resolutions. These features combined to make the Snapdragon 845 a comprehensive platform for the next generation of AI-powered mobile applications that would define the smartphone experience in 2018 and beyond.
