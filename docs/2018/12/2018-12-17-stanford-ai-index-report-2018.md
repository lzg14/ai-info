# Stanford AI Index Report 2018

The Stanford AI Index released its 2018 annual report in December, providing comprehensive documentation of the rapid acceleration in artificial intelligence development across the globe. The report, produced by the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) in collaboration with partners from MIT, OpenAI, Harvard, and McKinsey, presented a data-driven analysis of AI progress across academic research, industry applications, and policy considerations. What distinguished this year's report was its stronger emphasis on global data, recognizing that AI development had truly become a worldwide phenomenon with significant activity across North America, Europe, and Asia.

The report revealed striking findings about the global distribution of AI research output. In terms of AI-related academic papers indexed in Scopus, Europe led with 28% of global output, followed closely by China at 25% and the United States at 17%. However, despite the volume of Chinese publications, U.S. papers continued to enjoy the highest citation rates, indicating stronger research impact. Notably, Chinese AI paper citations had increased by 44% since 2006, demonstrating rapid quality improvements alongside quantity growth. At the 2018 AAAI conference, an impressive 70% of submitted papers originated from either the United States or China, highlighting the intense competition between these two AI superpowers.

Beyond research metrics, the report documented explosive growth in deep learning job demand, noting that positions requiring deep learning skills had increased 35-fold over the previous two years. The report highlighted the remarkable expansion of AI education, particularly at leading institutions like Tsinghua University, where enrollment in AI and machine learning courses had grown 16 times larger in 2017 compared to 2010. The 2018 AI Index report served as an important baseline for tracking AI's trajectory, establishing [Benchmark](../../../glossary/terms/benchmark.md)s that would be refined and expanded in subsequent years. Its comprehensive coverage and data-driven approach made it an essential reference for policymakers, researchers, and industry leaders seeking to understand the rapidly evolving AI landscape.
