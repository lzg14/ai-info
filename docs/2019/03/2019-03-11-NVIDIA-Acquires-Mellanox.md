# NVIDIA Acquires Mellanox for $6.9 Billion to Boost AI Computing Infrastructure

📅 2019-03-11 | 📎 NVIDIA官方新闻

<!-- 正文开始 -->
## Content Summary

In March 2019, NVIDIA announced the acquisition of Mellanox Technologies for $6.9 billion, marking one of the largest deals in the AI infrastructure space. Mellanox was a leading supplier of high-performance interconnect solutions used in data centers, providing technologies like InfiniBand and Ethernet networking that are critical for connecting thousands of GPUs and CPUs in large-scale AI training clusters. The acquisition was completed in November 2019 and significantly strengthened NVIDIA's position in the AI computing infrastructure market.

Before this acquisition, NVIDIA already dominated the GPU market for AI training, but large-scale AI deployments required sophisticated networking to efficiently coordinate thousands of processors. Mellanox's interconnect technology enabled direct GPU-to-GPU communication with ultra-low latency, which was essential for distributed training of models like BERT and GPT-2. Companies like Amazon, Microsoft, and Google relied on Mellanox hardware for their cloud AI infrastructure.

The strategic importance of this deal became even more apparent as the AI industry moved toward larger models requiring thousands of GPUs working in parallel. The combination of NVIDIA's GPUs with Mellanox's networking created an end-to-end platform for AI computing infrastructure. This acquisition signaled NVIDIA's ambition to control not just the computing hardware but also the networking fabric that connects AI systems together, positioning the company as a comprehensive AI infrastructure provider rather than merely a chip vendor.

This deal also reflected the intensifying competition among major tech companies to build superior AI infrastructure. As AI model training requirements grew exponentially, the ability to efficiently network large GPU clusters became a competitive advantage. The acquisition helped NVIDIA stay ahead of competitors like Intel and AMD in the AI hardware race, establishing a moat around its dominant GPU business through complementary networking technology.
