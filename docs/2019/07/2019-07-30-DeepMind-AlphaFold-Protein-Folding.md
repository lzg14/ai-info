# DeepMind's AlphaFold Solves Protein Folding Problem, Revolutionizing Computational Biology

📅 2019-07-30 | 📎 Nature

<!-- 正文开始 -->
DeepMind announced a breakthrough in protein structure prediction that sent ripples through the scientific community. The company's AlphaFold system, developed by its London-based team of AI researchers, achieved unprecedented accuracy in predicting the 3D structures of proteins from their amino acid sequences. This accomplishment addressed one of biology's grand challenges, known as the protein folding problem, which had stumped scientists for decades and carried implications for understanding diseases and developing new drugs.

Proteins are essential to virtually every biological process, and their functions are determined by their unique 3D structures. Determining these structures experimentally through techniques like X-ray crystallography and cryo-electron microscopy is expensive and time-consuming, often taking months or years per protein. AlphaFold could predict structures in hours or days, potentially transforming how researchers approach structural biology and accelerating the pace of biomedical discovery.

The team developed novel neural network architectures that could predict various properties of protein structures, including the distances between amino acid pairs and the angles of chemical bonds connecting them. By combining these predictions with physical models of protein structure, AlphaFold generated predictions that rivaled experimental results in accuracy. DeepMind participated in the CASP13 competition, a biennial assessment of protein structure prediction methods, where AlphaFold significantly outperformed other approaches.

The implications for medicine and drug discovery were substantial. Understanding protein structures helps researchers understand how diseases operate at the molecular level and design drugs that can interact specifically with disease-related proteins. Many genetic mutations cause diseases by altering protein structures, and the ability to predict these changes could accelerate research into rare diseases and personalized medicine approaches.

DeepMind committed to sharing their methods and collaborating with the scientific community to further improve protein structure prediction. The company released technical details of their approach and partnered with organizations to apply AlphaFold to challenges in disease research. Scientists around the world began exploring applications ranging from understanding the mechanisms of neurodegenerative diseases to engineering novel proteins for industrial applications.
