# GPT-3 Paper Published: Language Models are Few-Shot Learners

📅 2020-07-15 | 📎 arXiv

<!-- 正文开始 -->
OpenAI researchers published the landmark paper "Language Models are Few-Shot Learners" on arXiv, presenting GPT-3 in full technical detail. The paper described how the 175-billion-parameter model achieved state-of-the-art performance on a wide range of NLP tasks without requiring task-specific fine-tuning.

The research demonstrated that simply by scaling up language model training, models could generalize to new tasks with minimal examples through in-context learning. This represented a paradigm shift in how AI systems could be designed, as developers no longer needed to collect large labeled datasets for each specific task.

The paper showed GPT-3's impressive capabilities across diverse benchmarks including translation, question answering, cloze tasks, and news article generation. The model also revealed limitations, including difficulties with rare word tasks, common sense physics, and logical reasoning in certain contexts. These findings sparked discussions about the fundamental capabilities and limitations of scale in neural networks.

The publication was followed by intense interest from the research community, with hundreds of papers subsequently built on or around GPT-3's approach. The work established "scaling laws" as a central theme in AI research and inspired efforts to build even larger models while also investigating more efficient architectures.
