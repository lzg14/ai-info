# AlphaFold 2 Achieves Breakthrough at CASP14: A New Era for Structural Biology

📅 2020-11-30 | 📎 Nature

<!-- 正文开始 -->
The results from CASP14 (14th Critical Assessment of Protein Structure Prediction) confirmed that DeepMind's AlphaFold 2 had achieved a historic breakthrough in computational biology. AlphaFold 2 achieved median scores of around 92.5 GDT (Global Distance Test), effectively solving the protein folding problem with accuracy comparable to experimental techniques like X-ray crystallography.

This achievement was described by structural biologists as a "revolution" that would transform the field. For many proteins, particularly those involved in diseases like SARS-CoV-2, AlphaFold 2 could predict structures that would have taken years and enormous cost to determine experimentally. The system was particularly impressive for its ability to predict the structure of proteins with no known similar structures.

DeepMind's success came from applying state-of-the-art deep learning techniques, including attention mechanisms and novel architecture designs, to the specific problem of protein structure prediction. The system learned to interpret the relationship between amino acid sequences and their functional 3D conformations from vast amounts of biological data.

The scientific community responded with enthusiasm and calls for even broader access to the technology. Researchers envisioned applications ranging from understanding genetic mutations to designing new therapeutics. DeepMind committed to continuing to improve the system and exploring partnerships to maximize the benefit to scientific research worldwide.
