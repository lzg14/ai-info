# NVIDIA Launches DGX SuperPOD: Setting New Standards for AI Infrastructure

📅 2020-12-04 | 📎 NVIDIA Blog

<!-- 正文开始 -->
NVIDIA announced significant expansions to its DGX SuperPOD infrastructure, establishing new benchmarks for AI computing performance in data centers. The system could deliver unprecedented compute power for training the largest AI models, including language models like GPT-3 and scientific applications like protein folding prediction.

The DGX SuperPOD architecture allowed organizations to deploy thousands of GPUs working together on AI workloads. NVIDIA highlighted partnerships with major research institutions and tech companies deploying these systems to accelerate AI research and development. The infrastructure was specifically optimized for the computational demands of modern deep learning applications.

Key features included NVIDIA's InfiniBand networking for fast GPU-to-GPU communication, optimized software stacks for popular AI frameworks, and reference architectures for different deployment scenarios. NVIDIA positioned the DGX SuperPOD as essential infrastructure for organizations seeking to compete in AI development.

The announcement reflected the growing importance of computational resources in AI advancement. As models continued to grow in size and complexity, access to adequate computing infrastructure became a critical factor in AI research capabilities. NVIDIA's dominance in AI accelerators made it a central player in the global AI race.
