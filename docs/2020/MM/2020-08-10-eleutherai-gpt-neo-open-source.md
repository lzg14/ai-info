---
title: EleutherAI Launches GPT-Neo: Open Source Alternative to GPT-3
date: 2020-08-10
source: EleutherAI Blog
url: https://www.eleuther.ai/blog/gpt-neo-release/
---

EleutherAI, a grassroots collective of AI researchers and enthusiasts, announced the release of GPT-Neo, an open-source implementation of the GPT-3 architecture. This release represented a significant milestone in the movement toward democratizing access to large language models, providing the community with a freely available model that could compete with proprietary systems in certain tasks.

The project emerged from the recognition that the computational resources required to train state-of-the-art language models were beyond the reach of most researchers, academic institutions, and smaller organizations. GPT-Neo aimed to bridge this gap by providing pre-trained models that anyone could download, use, and modify without licensing restrictions or usage fees. The model came in two sizes: a 125 million parameter model and a 1.3 billion parameter model, named GPT-Neo and GPT-NeoX respectively.

The training process for GPT-Neo drew contributions from volunteers worldwide who donated computational resources through distributed computing initiatives. This crowdsourced approach to training demonstrated the potential for collaborative AI development outside traditional corporate or academic structures. The training data consisted of the Pile, a diverse 825GB dataset specifically curated for training large language models, which helped the model develop broad capabilities across different domains.

GPT-Neo's release generated substantial interest within the AI community, with researchers exploring applications ranging from code completion and text generation to creative writing and educational assistance. The open-source nature of the project enabled unprecedented transparency into how large language models behaved, allowing researchers to study potential biases, failure modes, and safety concerns that had been difficult to examine in closed proprietary systems.

The EleutherAI team expressed commitment to ongoing development and announced plans for larger models and improved training methodologies. They emphasized that GPT-Neo was just the beginning of a broader effort to ensure that advanced AI technology remained accessible to humanity as a whole, rather than being concentrated among a small number of technology companies with massive computational resources.
