# OpenAI Releases DALL-E Text-to-Image Generator

📅 2021-01-05

<!-- 正文开始 -->
In January 2021, OpenAI unveiled DALL-E, a groundbreaking AI system capable of generating images from textual descriptions. The name is a portmanteau of Salvador Dalí, the renowned surrealist painter, and WALL-E, the beloved robot from the Pixar film. DALL-E represents a novel neural network specifically designed for image generation, based on the [GPT](../../glossary/terms/gpt.md)-3 architecture but using a smaller 120-billion parameter version rather than GPT-3's 175 billion. The model was trained on text-image pairs dataset, allowing it to create images from natural language descriptions in a way that mirrors how GPT-3 generates websites and stories.

DALL-E can create extremely realistic and clear images based on simple text prompts, demonstrating mastery of various artistic styles including illustrations and landscapes. It can also generate images with text to create building signs, and produce both sketches and full-color images of the same scene. What makes DALL-E particularly remarkable is its ability to combine unrelated concepts in creative ways, generating images that would be impossible or highly impractical for human artists to create. For example, users can describe "an armchair in the shape of an avocado" or "a teapot in the shape of a penguin," and DALL-E will generate appropriate images.

The system was recognized as one of the ten most breakthrough digital innovation technologies selected by the Netexplo university network after a year of global selection. In April 2021, just months after its launch, DALL-E was included in this prestigious list, acknowledging its revolutionary impact on the intersection of artificial intelligence and creative arts. The technology demonstrated that AI could move beyond simple pattern recognition to genuine creative expression, opening new possibilities for artists, designers, and creators of all kinds. While the initial version had resolution and latency limitations, it laid the foundation for what would become DALL-E 2, launched in 2022 with four times the resolution and additional image editing capabilities.
