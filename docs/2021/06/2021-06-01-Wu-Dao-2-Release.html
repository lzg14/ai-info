Wu Dao 2.0 1.75 Trillion Parameters

Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence2021-06-01入库 2021-06-01

The Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence unveiled Wu Dao 2.0 in June 2021, claiming the model surpassed GPT-3 with 1.75 trillion parameters. This massive Chinese AI model represented a significant milestone in the global competition for AI supremacy and demonstrated China's rapidly advancing capabilities in large language models.

Wu Dao 2.0 combined multiple AI technologies including multimodal understanding, text generation, image recognition, and prediction capabilities. The model could process text, images, and video simultaneously, positioning it as a true multimodal AI system comparable to or exceeding existing Western counterparts.

The training approach incorporated Chinese cultural and linguistic elements, positioning the model as particularly suited for Chinese language tasks. However, the system also supported English and other languages, making it globally competitive for international applications.

Technical innovations included FastMoE, an efficient mixture-of-experts implementation that enabled training across thousands of GPUs while maintaining communication efficiency. This open-source contribution to the PyTorch ecosystem benefited the broader AI research community.

The release sparked discussions about AI competition between the United States and China. Wu Dao 2.0 demonstrated that major AI capabilities were no longer exclusively in the domain of American companies, with Chinese research institutions achieving competitive results in foundational AI research.
