DeepMind announced a historic breakthrough in July 2021 with AlphaFold2, an AI system that achieved unprecedented accuracy in predicting protein structures. The announcement came during the biennial Critical Assessment of Protein Structure Prediction (CASP14) competition, where AlphaFold2 demonstrated performance that rivaled experimental methods in determining the 3D structures of proteins. This achievement was hailed by the scientific community as one of the most important advances in biology and chemistry in decades.

Proteins are the building blocks of life, and their function is determined by their 3D structure. Understanding these structures has been crucial for drug discovery, understanding diseases, and developing new biotechnology applications. However, determining protein structures experimentally has been extremely time-consuming and expensive, often taking years for a single protein. AlphaFold2 could predict these structures in hours or minutes, potentially revolutionizing biomedical research.

The system used a novel architecture based on attention mechanisms, similar to those used in large language models, but adapted for processing amino acid sequences and predicting spatial relationships. DeepMind trained AlphaFold2 on known protein structures from the Protein Data Bank and used massive computational resources to achieve its results. The model could not only predict the final structure but also provide confidence estimates for each part of the prediction.

DeepMind and the European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL) later released the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database, making over 200 million protein structure predictions freely available to the scientific community. This database has since been used by researchers worldwide to accelerate work on drug discovery, enzyme design, and understanding the mechanisms of diseases. The impact of AlphaFold2 was recognized with numerous awards and was featured as a breakthrough of the year in multiple scientific publications.
