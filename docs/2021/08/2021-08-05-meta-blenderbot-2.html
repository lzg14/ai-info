Meta AI Releases BlenderBot 2.0 with Internet Search

Meta AI Blog2021-08-05入库 2021-08-05

Meta AI announced BlenderBot 2.0 in August 2021, an advanced conversational AI system that incorporated real-time internet search capabilities to maintain long-term memory and discuss current topics. Building on the original BlenderBot released in 2020, the new version addressed one of the major limitations of previous chatbots: their inability to discuss events or information beyond their training data. By integrating internet search, BlenderBot 2.0 could look up current information and incorporate it into conversations.

The system used a three-component architecture: a neural retriever that found relevant information from the internet based on the conversation context, a neural ranker that selected the most appropriate pieces of information, and a generator that incorporated the retrieved information into natural responses. This approach allowed the chatbot to discuss recent news, provide updated recommendations, and avoid the "hallucination" problem where chatbots would confidently state incorrect information.

BlenderBot 2.0 also featured improved persona and empathy capabilities, maintaining consistency in its character throughout extended conversations. The system could remember facts about the user and reference previous discussion points, creating a more coherent and engaging conversational experience. Meta released the model and code to the research community, enabling others to build on and improve the technology.

The release represented Meta's continued investment in open-source conversational AI research and its commitment to advancing the field toward more useful and engaging chatbots. The combination of retrieval and generation addressed fundamental limitations of purely generative models and pointed toward hybrid architectures as a promising direction for future dialogue systems. Researchers and developers could access the models through Meta's GitHub repository and Hugging Face.
