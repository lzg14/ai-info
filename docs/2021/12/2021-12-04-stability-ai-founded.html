Stability AI emerged from stealth in December 2021 as a new force in the AI industry, positioning itself as a provider of open-source AI infrastructure. Founded by Emad Mostaque, the company aimed to democratize access to AI technology through open development and community collaboration.

The company gained immediate prominence through its support of Stable Diffusion, a text-to-image diffusion model that would release in 2022. By providing computational resources and open-source development frameworks, Stability AI enabled a global community of researchers and developers to contribute to cutting-edge AI systems.

Stability AI's model differed from proprietary AI companies by emphasizing open development and transparency. All code and model weights were publicly available, allowing anyone to inspect, modify, or deploy AI systems without licensing restrictions or API dependencies.

The company's mission centered on enabling nations, organizations, and individuals to harness AI technology according to their own values and needs. This decentralized approach to AI development contrasted with concentrated AI power among a few technology giants.

Stability AI's emergence signaled a shift toward more open and accessible AI development. By 2022, the company had grown to hundreds of employees and raised significant funding while maintaining its commitment to open-source principles and broad AI accessibility.
