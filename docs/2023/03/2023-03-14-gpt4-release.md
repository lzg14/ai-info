# OpenAI GPT-4 Release Marks New Era in AI Capabilities

📅 2023-03-14

<!-- 正文开始 -->
On March 14, 2023, OpenAI officially released GPT-4, marking a significant milestone in the development of large language models. As the successor to GPT-3.5, which powered ChatGPT, GPT-4 brought dramatic improvements in reasoning, creativity, and instruction following. The model demonstrated remarkable abilities to understand and process both text and images, making it the first multimodal GPT series model available to the public.

GPT-4 exhibited substantial advances across multiple dimensions. In terms of factual accuracy, GPT-4 performed significantly better than its predecessors, reducing [Hallucination](../../glossary/terms/hallucination.md)s by approximately 40% compared to [GPT-3.5](../../glossary/terms/gpt.md). The model scored in the top 10 percentile on the Uniform Bar Examination, achieving a score that would qualify a human to practice law in most jurisdictions. It also scored in the 90th percentile on the GRE verbal exam and excelled at complex reasoning tasks in science, mathematics, and programming.

One of the most significant improvements in GPT-4 was its ability to handle nuanced instructions and maintain context over much longer conversations. The context window was expanded to 8,192 tokens, with a 32,768-token version available through the API. This enabled developers to build more sophisticated applications ranging from legal document analysis to educational tutoring systems.

OpenAI emphasized that GPT-4 underwent extensive safety testing and red-teaming before release. The company implemented new techniques to make the model's responses more aligned with human values, though challenges remained in preventing all forms of misuse. GPT-4 was made available through [ChatGPT](../../glossary/terms/chatgpt.md) Plus subscription and the OpenAI API, catalyzing a wave of innovation in AI-powered applications across industries.
