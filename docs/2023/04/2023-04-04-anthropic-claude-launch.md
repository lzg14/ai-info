# Anthropic Launches Claude AI Assistant with Constitutional AI

Anthropic officially launched Claude, their next-generation AI assistant, in April 2023, introducing innovative safety techniques that distinguished it from other conversational AI systems. Named after the classic science fiction novel "The Yellow Wallpaper" character, Claude embodied Anthropic's vision of developing helpful, harmless, and honest AI. The launch marked Anthropic's entry into the competitive consumer AI market.

Claude was built using Constitutional AI, a novel training methodology developed by Anthropic researchers. This approach incorporated a set of guiding principles that the model learned to follow through self-critique and revision, reducing harmful outputs while maintaining usefulness. The technique represented a significant advance in AI [Alignment](../../../glossary/terms/alignment.md), addressing concerns about [AI Safety](../../../glossary/terms/ai-safety.md) without sacrificing capability.

The model demonstrated strong performance across a wide range of tasks including conversation, analysis, creative writing, coding, and reasoning. [Claude](../../../glossary/terms/claude.md) was available through a web interface at claude.ai and via API for developers. Anthropic emphasized the assistant's ability to engage with complex topics thoughtfully, refuse harmful requests clearly, and explain its reasoning when appropriate.

Anthropic's launch of Claude came amid growing regulatory scrutiny of AI systems. The company's focus on safety and transparency resonated with enterprises concerned about reputational risk and regulatory compliance. Claude found particular adoption in sectors requiring high reliability and ethical operation, including healthcare, legal services, and finance. The release demonstrated that safety and capability could be developed together, influencing the broader AI industry's approach to responsible development.
